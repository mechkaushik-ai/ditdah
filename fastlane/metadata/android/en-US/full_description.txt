Dit Dah turns anything you type into International Morse Code, and plays it as clean beeps, flashlight blinks or vibrations.

<b>Send</b>
• Type a message and hear it in Morse, with each letter highlighted as it plays
• Play through the speaker, the flashlight, vibration, or all three at once
• Adjust speed from 5 to 40 words per minute and tone from 400 to 1000 Hz
• Learning mode (Farnsworth spacing): full-speed letters with extra space between them
• Share the written Morse code to any app, or share text from any app into Dit Dah

<b>Decode</b>
• Type or paste dots and dashes and see the text instantly
• Listen through the microphone and decode Morse tones live, with automatic speed tracking
• Practise with the on-screen tap key: hear each dot and dash as you key it
• Speed, tone and volume controls on every screen

<b>Chart</b>
• All letters, numbers and punctuation in the International (ITU) standard
• Tap any character to hear it

<b>Private by design</b>
• Works completely offline
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• Collects no personal data

Dit Dah is free and open source.
